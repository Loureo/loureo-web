# Loureo · product and voice

What Loureo is, who it talks to and how it sounds. Read before writing any copy or designing any page.
Visual rules live in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).

## What Loureo is

Loureo is an innovation lab in Madrid, Spain. It explores new ways of working with technology (today, working
alongside AI agents) and turns the ideas that hold up into real things: prototypes, tools and products, hardware or
software, from the first prototype to the box. It started as a software studio (client work, AI integration); the
website no longer sells services. It presents the lab and what comes out of it.

Say "innovation lab" (ES: "laboratorio de innovación"), never "hardware studio", "agency" or "startup". A lab here
means rigour and finished work, not a playground of half-done experiments: only what is real goes on the site.

Reference points (for stance, not for copying): Modem (modemworks.com), Work Louder, Teenage Engineering, and Anthropic's
own site for tone. A serious lab, not a startup: no hype, no growth language, no "revolutionary".

## Who it is for

People who work with computers all day, and increasingly with AI agents. They are curious about tools, care about
objects on their desk, and have no patience for spec sheets that say nothing. Spanish and English speakers (the site
is EN + ES).

## Values

From Loureo's first manifesto. The **titles stay in English in both languages** (they are the lab's mottos); the
descriptions are translated.

1. **Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication.** Less is more. Technology you notice by how little it asks of you.
2. **Mind, heart and guts.** Analysis, empathy and intuition. People before technique.
3. **Flexible like gravity.** Firm where it matters, adaptable everywhere else.
4. **Always focus on future and offbeat.** Curiosity first. We take the unusual path when it leads somewhere better.
5. **Making mistakes makes us grow.** We prototype, measure and try again. Fail fast, learn fast.
6. **Tech is for and about people.** It should make life simpler, not busier. We build it with the people who use it.

Purpose (footer): "Technology should make life simpler, not busier. We test new ideas until they do, and build the ones
that earn a place in your day."

## Voice

- **Say what it does for you, not what it is made of.** The model is Apple's "1,000 songs in your pocket" against a
  competitor's "5 GB of storage". "Answer without looking. One turn, one click." beats "Spun aluminium wheel with a
  magnetic encoder". Specs belong on the specs page only.
- **Few words.** Cut subtitles that repeat the title. If a line could be deleted without losing anything, delete it.
  No sentence "because it sounds nice".
- **Concrete and specific.** Times, names, actions: "14:26 · It needs you. Before emailing it to Ana, it asks."
- **Calm and sure.** Short declaratives. No exclamation marks, no rhetorical questions, no "imagine…".
- **Never claim what is not true yet.** Almenara is not for sale; it has a waitlist. Do not invent prices, dates,
  certifications or press.
- Avoid: "revolutionary", "game-changer", "seamless", "empower", "leverage", "cutting-edge", "AI-powered" as an adjective,
  em-dash chains, "It's not X, it's Y".

## Language

- English is the reference and lives in the HTML; Spanish is applied on top (`data-i18n` keys, dictionary per page).
- Location is always written "Madrid, Spain" ("Madrid, España" in Spanish), never just "Spain".
- Spanish is natural Spanish from Spain, not a word-for-word translation ("Giras. Pulsas. Listo.").
- Repo content (code, comments, docs, commit messages) is in English.
