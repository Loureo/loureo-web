What we believe.

  • Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication

    Less is more. Technology you notice by how little it asks of you.

  • Mind, heart and guts

    Analysis, empathy and intuition. People before technique.

  • Flexible like gravity

    Firm where it matters, adaptable everywhere else.

  • Always focus on future and offbeat

    Curiosity first. We take the unusual path when it leads somewhere better.

  • Making mistakes makes us grow

    We prototype, measure and try again. Fail fast, learn fast.

  • Tech is for and about people

    It should make life simpler, not busier. We build it with the people who use it.
