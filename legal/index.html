This notice covers loureo.com and its pages, as required by Spain's Law 34/2002 on information society services (LSSI). The content, images and code of this site belong to Loureo SL. All rights reserved.

Your data

We only collect personal data when you join a waitlist: your email address and the language you were reading in.

What we use it for

To write to you about that project: when there is something to show and when it can be ordered. Nothing else, and we never sell or share it for anyone else's marketing.

Why we may

Your consent, given when you submit the form (Art. 6.1.a GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time.

Who handles it for us

Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France) keeps the list and sends the emails, with data stored in the European Union. Vercel Inc. hosts the site and keeps short-lived server logs, which include IP addresses; it transfers data to the United States under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Both act on our instructions under a data processing agreement.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, or until the waitlist closes, whichever comes first.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or take away your data, to limit or object to its use, and unsubscribe with the link in any of our emails. Write to hello@loureo.com. If you think we have not handled your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. Your browser keeps two small notes for the site itself: the language you chose, and whether you have already seen the opening card today. They never leave your device.
